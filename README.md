Waking up sounds easy. Your alarm rings, you turn it off, and you get on with your day.

Well... at least that's how it's supposed to work. Sometimes, turning off the alarm is the easiest part. Getting out of bed is a completely different story.

That's where Walkup comes in.

## What is Walkup?

Walkup is an alarm app built around a simple idea: **what if waking up meant actually getting up?**

Most alarm apps do their job by making noise until you dismiss the alarm. Walkup takes inspiration from something a little different — moving around instead of staying in bed.

The idea behind Walkup is pretty straightforward. Your alarm is the start of your morning, not the finish line. Rather than making the whole experience about tapping a button, Walkup puts the focus on getting up and moving.

It's a small change in how we think about alarms, but it's the whole point of the project.

## 🚶 Walk to Wake Up

This is what Walkup is all about.

**Walk to Wake Up** isn't just a tagline. It's the idea that inspired the app in the first place.

Think about it: your alarm goes off, you reach for your phone, tap dismiss, and five minutes later you're right back where you started. Sound familiar? Yeah, we've all been there.

Walkup is built around a different approach. Instead of treating waking up as nothing more than stopping an alarm, it connects the experience with movement.

Getting out of bed and walking around gives you a reason to do something instead of immediately settling back into your pillow. The goal isn't to make mornings harder than they need to be. It's to make getting up feel like an actual part of the alarm experience.

No complicated morning routine. No promises that you'll suddenly become the most productive person alive. Just a simple idea: when it's time to wake up, take that first step.

**Don't just turn off your alarm. Walk to wake up.**

## Why Walkup?

There are already plenty of alarm apps out there, so why make another one?

Because the way we wake up is worth rethinking.

An alarm can get your attention, but that doesn't always mean you're ready to leave your bed. Walkup explores how a little movement can become part of that transition.

The goal is to keep things focused. An alarm app doesn't need to do a hundred different things to have a clear identity. Sometimes, one good idea is enough to build a project around.

That's what Walkup is trying to do.

## Built Around a Simple Idea

Walkup is for anyone who wants a different approach to waking up — whether you're a student trying to get ready for school, someone working on a more consistent routine, or just someone who really likes their sleep.

It's not going to magically fix your sleep schedule, and it's not pretending to. The idea is much simpler than that: give yourself a reason to get moving when your alarm goes off.

Small habits take time to build, and everybody has their own way of starting the day. Walkup is simply another way to approach that first moment in the morning.

## A Little More About the Name

Walkup comes from putting two ideas together: *walk* and *wake up*.

It's simple, easy to remember, and pretty much explains the concept on its own. The name isn't about walking long distances or turning your morning into a workout. It's about that small moment when you stop lying in bed and actually start moving.

That's the part Walkup cares about.
